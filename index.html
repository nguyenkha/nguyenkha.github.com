Portfolio

There are two sentences I say too often each year: "this tool is almost right… but not quite" and "wait, I’m paying for this?" 🤔 The apps below are my answer. A "K" up front — same move Monty pulled when he named MySQL after My. Free, forever.

kexify

A self-custodial MPC wallet for secure digital asset management.

Built after years inside major virtual-asset exchanges, where one recurring thought kept showing up: security should feel effortless, not intimidating.

kexify.co

KAN 杯 PAY

A clean, social-first app for splitting shared expenses.

This one started the moment our group looked at the Splitwise Pro bill and collectively said, “nope.” 💸

kanpay.app

pdf.kha.do

Compress PDFs privately, right in your browser.

Your document stays on your device while the browser does the work.

pdf.kha.do

fly

A privacy-first scanner that decodes airline boarding passes right in your browser.

Born from one airport-line question — "what's actually hidden in this barcode?" — answered without my name or PNR ever leaving the phone. ✈️🔒

fly.kha.do

konfirm

Paste a stuck Bitcoin, Ethereum, Polygon, Base or Avalanche transaction and see why it is pending, plus live fees.

Fee advice online is mostly a guess, so this one is scored against what blocks actually include, with the test written down before the results came in. ⛓️📏

konfirm.kha.do

delay

Type a flight number and a date to see the chance it leaves more than 15 minutes late, and by how much.

For everyone who has sprinted to a gate only to wait an hour beside it. ✈️⏱️

delay.kha.do
